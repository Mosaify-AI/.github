## What we're building

Mosaify is being built for the full creative loop—not a single model or one-off generation. It brings the expanding AI video landscape into a consistent workspace where creators can:

- begin with a prompt, image, reference, or existing project;
- generate through the model best suited to the work;
- organize projects, scenes, references, and outputs;
- keep creative context intact while iterating; and
- move from an initial idea toward a finished video.

## How we think

- **Model-agnostic by design.** Models change quickly; the creative workflow should remain coherent.
- **Direction over roulette.** References, context, and explicit choices should make iteration intentional.
- **One connected system.** Web, API, CLI, and agent-facing tools should share the same product truth.
- **Infrastructure should earn trust.** Reliability, privacy, and clear operational ownership are part of the product.
